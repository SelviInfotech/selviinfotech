A website is not a brochure that stays correct because nobody touched it. It is a small piece of software, a host, a certificate, and a set of words that describe your business. Each of those can go stale on its own. Selvi Infotech maintains sites for businesses worldwide. This note is the practical version of why that work exists.

## What goes wrong when nobody is assigned

You do not need a dramatic failure to have a site that is quietly wrong.

- The contact form still submits, and the messages arrive in an inbox nobody opens.
- A phone number or a price changed in the shop and not on the page.
- A framework or a library published a security fix, and the site is still on the old version because updating it was nobody's job.
- The TLS certificate, the domain, or the hosting invoice lapsed.
- A page that used to load a map, a font, or an embedded form now shows a hole, because the other service changed its rules.
- Someone can no longer log in to the host, because the person who set it up left.

None of these require a redesign. They require a person who is allowed to look, on a schedule you both understand.

## What we mean by maintenance

On a [web maintenance](/services/web-maintenance/) engagement we agree a list. Typical items are dependency updates, defects you report, small content and layout edits, a check that backups exist, and a check that the contact path still reaches a person.

A new section, a new product, or a rebuild is not maintenance. Those are [web development](/services/web-development/) or [software development](/services/software-development/), scoped on their own. If everything is called maintenance, you cannot tell whether the retainer is being used.

We work in React, Next.js, Node.js, ASP.NET Core, Python, Java Spring Boot, and SQL Server, on Azure or another ordinary host. We also take on sites we did not build, after we have seen the code. If the stack is outside that list, we say so before we take the keys.

## What maintenance is not

It is not a promise that you will rank higher on Google. A site that is down, slow, or describing last year's offer is harder to trust, and harder for a search engine to represent accurately. Keeping it accurate protects that chance. It does not buy a position. We will not sell a retainer as a ranking package.

It is also not an unnamed 24-hour desk unless the agreement says that. Cosmetic edits wait. A site that is down, a form that stopped delivering, or a security fix that should not wait are the items we treat first. The response you can expect is the one written down, not an assumption.

## What we need from you before we start

The repository or the files. Access to the host. A person who can approve a change the same week, not three months later. Without those, maintenance is a standing meeting about a site we cannot touch.

If you are not sure the site is worth keeping, that is a fair question. A short [consulting](/services/it-consulting/) review can end in "leave it", "repair it", or "replace it". Replacing it is a new build. Repairing it is the retainer.
