FAQ

Common Questions

Are the certificates recognised by employers?

Yes. Our certificates are issued by Selvi Infotech Private Limited and can be instantly verified by employers at selviinfotech.com/verify.html. We also provide LinkedIn-shareable certificates on request.

Do I need prior experience to join?

It depends on the course. Python, Full Stack Web Development, and UI/UX are beginner-friendly. Mobile App Development, Cloud, and Data Science work best with some programming exposure. Contact us and we'll guide you to the right path.

Are classes held online or in person?

Most programs are available both online (live sessions) and in-person at our Tiruvallur, Tamil Nadu centre. Cloud Computing is primarily online due to lab infrastructure.

Is placement assistance provided?

Yes. Eligible students on our Full Stack and Data Science programs receive resume review, mock interviews, and referrals to our hiring partner network.

How do I enrol?

Fill in the contact form on our website, or WhatsApp us at +91 9500316716. Our team will share the batch schedule, fee structure, and next steps within 24 hours.
